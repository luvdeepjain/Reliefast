
# TMJ Pen — Mechanical Design Report

## 1. Objective

To develop a compact, ergonomic device concept incorporating
heat, vibration, and targeted acupressure for jaw muscle comfort.

## 2. Design Approach

The product is being developed using a CAD-first workflow.
SolidWorks is used to model the product geometry, casing, and
mechanical interfaces before completing the physical assembly.

## 3. Mechanical Design

The design work focuses on:

- Ergonomic grip and handling
- Compact casing geometry
- Tip geometry and contact interface
- Internal space for proposed electronic components
- Assembly access and maintainability
- Suitability for 3D printing

## 4. Manufacturing Approach

The prototype casing is being developed using 3D printing.
Bambu Lab printing equipment is used for rapid prototyping.

Final material selection and print settings should be
documented after confirming the actual manufacturing process.

## 5. Current Status

CAD files are available and the physical prototype is
partially assembled. Electronic integration has not yet
been completed.

## 6. Next Steps

1. Review CAD dimensions and component clearances.
2. Complete the mechanical assembly.
3. Confirm component specifications.
4. Integrate the electronics and control system.
5. Perform appropriate thermal and electrical safety tests.
6. Document measured results and design revisions.

## 7. Limitations

The prototype is under development. Clinical effectiveness
and suitability for therapeutic use have not been established.
