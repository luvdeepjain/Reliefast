## Recording Results

For every completed test, record:

- Test date
- Equipment used
- Test procedure
- Target value and acceptance criteria
- Actual measured result
- Pass/fail status
- Observations and corrective actions

Do not mark a test as passed until it has been performed
and the measured result satisfies its acceptance criteria.
